The Challenge

2000 Cigars is run by Vancouver's only Master Habanos Sommelier — a credential that carries real weight in-store, but the website itself was essentially invisible in search. Most demand still relied on walk-in trust rather than online discovery.

  • Thin content and missing on-page SEO fundamentals (H1s, meta descriptions, alt text)
  • No blog or educational content to capture top-of-funnel search intent
  • Online sales represented only 5% of total sales despite strong brand credibility
  • E-commerce for tobacco products is restricted in Canada, limiting direct online sales
My Role

SEO Specialist · Landing Page Design

My deliverables covered the full top-of-funnel: making the brand findable, then converting visitors once they arrived.

  • Ran keyword research and an on-page technical audit
  • Designed a blog content strategy ("Cigar 101")
  • Built local SEO recommendations
  • Designed a conversion-focused landing page mockup
  • Ran competitive and SWOT analysis
The Approach

SEO Strategy

Four workstreams, built to close the gap between in-store credibility and online discoverability.

Keyword Research
Identified high-intent keywords around premium cigars, Vancouver cigar shops, and Habanos brands — mapped to existing and new pages to close content gaps versus competitors.
On-Page Technical Fixes
Audited site structure and flagged missing H1 tags, thin content pages (under 300 words), unoptimized meta descriptions, and image alt-text gaps.
"Cigar 101" Blog Series
Designed an education-driven content series — "How to choose a cigar," "What is a Habanos sommelier" — each mapped to a keyword cluster and buying-intent stage.
Local SEO
Optimized for "cigar shop Vancouver" and nearby-intent queries — Google Business Profile recommendations, NAP consistency, and local citation building.

Landing Page & Visual Direction

A landing page mockup built to capture and convert organic traffic — lead-gen form, product showcase, and a brand credibility section built around the Master Habanos Sommelier certification.

Competitive & SWOT analysis rounded out the strategy: 3 Vancouver cigar retailers were audited on SEO presence, content depth, and keyword rankings — strengths (a unique Habanos sommelier credential), weaknesses (low traffic, thin content), an opportunity (no competitor ranking for education content), and a threat (Canada's e-commerce restrictions on tobacco).

The Results

Discoverable
A keyword and on-page plan built to close the visibility gap against local competitors.
Conversion-Ready

Positioned to Grow
A content roadmap ("Cigar 101") giving the brand a reason to rank beyond its own product pages.
What I Learned

SEO for a business with real-world constraints — like tobacco e-commerce restrictions — taught me that growth strategy has to work within the rules a business actually operates under, not around them. The goal wasn't to force an online store; it was to build a discovery funnel that respected those limits while still driving measurable intent.

  • In-store credibility and online discoverability are separate problems that need separate strategies
  • A content plan is only useful if each piece is mapped to a real keyword and a real funnel stage
  • Constraints (like regulatory limits) should shape the strategy, not be treated as an afterthought

The Challenge

Operations were previously managed through manual documents and disconnected workflows, creating data inconsistencies and limited visibility into labor efficiency, sales performance, and product profitability.

  • Manual data entry created inconsistencies and errors
  • Weekly reporting required manual consolidation
  • Labor and sales data were difficult to evaluate together
  • Product-level COGS and margin were not systematically tracked

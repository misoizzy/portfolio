The Challenge

Franchise clients needed a steady flow of qualified leads to support fast store expansion — but campaigns were spread across three ad platforms with different reporting formats, and acquisition costs were eating into franchise economics.

  • Meta, Google Ads, and Naver campaigns needed to run in parallel across 23+ clients
  • High customer acquisition costs were limiting franchise growth budgets
  • Lead volume needed to scale to support 9+ new store openings per month
  • No standardized landing page or funnel structure across clients
My Role

Marketing & Consultant

  • Directed full-cycle marketing for 6 F&B and retail franchise brands
  • Wrote proposals, ran, and optimized paid campaigns across Meta, Google, and Naver
  • Designed all ad creative in Photoshop & Illustrator
  • Optimized landing pages to lower cost per acquisition
  • Compiled weekly and monthly performance reports across platforms
The Approach

Multi-Channel Funnel

Every client ran the same core funnel — platform-native ads feeding into a landing page built to convert, monitored and adjusted weekly.

What I Learned

Running 23+ client campaigns at once taught me that performance marketing at scale is a systems problem as much as a creative one — the biggest cost reductions came from fixing the landing page and funnel, not just tweaking ad targeting.

  • The landing page is often the highest-leverage part of the funnel to optimize, not the ad itself
  • Consistent weekly monitoring catches problems (like a CTR drop) before they become expensive
  • Standardizing reporting across platforms made it possible to manage dozens of clients without losing signal

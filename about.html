Data & Systems

I started in marketing — learning how campaigns move people and how brands earn attention. At V WORKS (VMARK), a Daejeon-based ad agency, I built Meta and Naver campaign proposals for 30+ F&B franchise clients, ran the ads, and wrote monthly reports that explained why the numbers moved — not just what they were.

That instinct for data pulled me toward what happens behind the campaign — how a business actually operates. Completing a digital marketing co-op at Cornerstone College, Vancouver (Sep 2025 – Aug 2026) sharpened my approach to SEO, content strategy, SMART goal-setting, and structured decision-making, all in an English-language environment.

Today I combine marketing thinking with operational problem-solving. At La Forêt, I designed and built an internal ERP system for a 3-branch café operation, then used it to analyze labor cost against revenue. The pattern is the same at any scale: find the gap, build the fix, prove it with data.
